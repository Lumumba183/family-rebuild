Building Stronger Families Through Faith

Family Rebuild is a Faith-based organisation that exists to offer solid marital and family support. We offer biblical advice and use testimonies of those who have run the race and kept the faith despite the tough marriage and family terrain.

We have used this platform to reach many churches, schools and stratified groups with all-time rich marital teachings and family edification. Our main office is in Kiambu County, but we serve wider constituencies within Kenya and beyond.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our ministry, books, and services

How can I order a book from Family Rebuild?

You can order any of our books by filling out the contact form on this website, sending us a WhatsApp message, or emailing us at familyrebuild2026@gmail.com. We offer both hard copies and soft copies (e-books). Delivery is available across Kenya.

Do you offer marriage counseling services?

Yes! We offer biblical marriage counseling for couples at all stages of their relationship. Whether you are preparing for marriage, experiencing challenges, or simply want to strengthen your bond, our counseling sessions are grounded in Scripture and practical wisdom. Contact us to schedule a session.

Can you come and speak at our church or event?

Absolutely! Rev. Peter and Dr. Jane Wangaruro are available for speaking engagements at churches, schools, conferences, and community events. We have reached many churches and stratified groups across Kenya with rich marital teachings and family edification. Reach out to discuss your event.

Are your books available in digital format?

Yes, soft copies (e-books/PDF) are available for most of our titles. This is perfect for international readers or those who prefer digital reading. When ordering, simply indicate your preference for a digital copy in the order form.

How can I support the Family Rebuild ministry?

You can support us by purchasing our books, sharing our content with friends and family, inviting us to speak at your events, subscribing to our YouTube channel, and praying for the ministry. Your support helps us reach more families with the message of hope and restoration.
